# Kenyan money cases

These are where beginners' numbers usually go wrong. For each case: how to log it, and whether to ask.

| Situation | How to log it | Ask? |
|---|---|---|
| **Fuliza draw** ("OverDraft of Credit Party") | Borrowed money | No |
| **Fuliza repayment** ("OD Loan Repayment") | Mobile loans (debt) | No |
| **Fuliza access or daily fee** (in the Fuliza SMS) | Not logged on its own: it's added to the Fuliza balance and paid inside the repayment | No |
| **A purchase paid with Fuliza** ("Merchant Payment Fuliza M-Pesa", "Customer Transfer Fuliza MPesa") | By who was paid (shop, person). The overdraft line beside it is the Borrowed money | No |
| **"Salary Payment from …" / "Business Payment from …" on M-Pesa** | M-Pesa's label for payments from banks and companies. Could be salary, own bank money, an investment withdrawal, a loan or a refund | Yes, once per payer |
| **Money from your own bank "via API"** (Standard Chartered, Absa, NCBA, I&M, Equity) | Transfer between my accounts, and add that bank to the Month-End Check | Once per bank |
| **Bond coupon** ("O/A COUP IFB…" on a bank statement) | Investment income (starter rule COUP) | No |
| **Ziidi MMF / unit trust round-ups** (many tiny deposits) | Money market fund, grouped into one row a month by the tool | No |
| **Tala / Branch / Zenka / Hustler Fund loan paid to you** | Borrowed money | No; add the loan to My Loans if missing |
| **Mobile loan repayment** | Mobile loans | No |
| **M-Shwari / KCB M-Pesa / Lock Savings deposit** | Their savings category (or the emergency fund if that's where the fund sits) | Once: "Is M-Shwari your emergency fund or other savings?" |
| **M-Shwari / MMF / SACCO withdrawal to M-Pesa** | From my savings (or From my emergency fund) | Once |
| **M-Shwari loan** | Borrowed money in, Mobile loans out | No |
| **Chama contribution** | Chama (savings) | First time |
| **Chama payout (your turn)** | From my savings, since it's your own savings coming back | First time; explain why it isn't income |
| **Chama loan** | Borrowed money in, the debt category out | Yes |
| **Reversal** | Cancels the original; nothing logged | No (the script pairs them) |
| **Refund from a shop** | Cancels the purchase | Only if it can't be paired |
| **Lending to a friend** | Gifts & celebrations if you don't expect it back; otherwise Miscellaneous with Note "lent to …" | Yes; talk about it kindly |
| **Friend repays you** | Gifts received, or match the earlier Note | Yes |
| **Salary advance** | Borrowed money; the deduction next month goes to a debt category | Yes |
| **Sending money home / family support** | Family support (a Need unless they say otherwise) | Once per person, then a rule |
| **Diaspora: earning in GBP/USD/EUR** | Convert at a rate they give; write the rate in the Note; label ESTIMATED | Ask the rate every month |
| **Diaspora: Wise / WorldRemit / Sendwave** | Family support or Transfer between my accounts (if it's their own Kenyan account) | Yes |
| **Business and personal on one line** | Business sales: Business income; personal spending: usual categories; stock and suppliers: ask | Yes. Suggest a separate business line and paying themselves a fixed "salary" |
| **Many small customer payments** | One merged question, then a rule (e.g. "MERCHANT CUSTOMER PAYMENT" → Business income) | Once |
| **Cash-heavy life** | Agent withdrawals: Cash withdrawal; then ask for a rough weekly cash split (food, transport, …) and log it ESTIMATED | Yes |
| **Airtime bought for others** | Gifts & celebrations or Family support | Only if large |
| **Okoa Jahazi / airtime credit** | Borrowed money when given; Airtime & data when repaid | No |
| **Betting (SportPesa, Betika, etc.)** | Fun & entertainment, with a gentle note | See boundaries if frequent or large |
| **Joint or family account** | Log only their share; Note "joint" | Yes: what share is theirs |
| **NSSF / SHIF / PAYE on payslip** | Not on statements, because take-home pay is already after these. Track pension value on My Net Worth / My Future | No |
| **Rent received from tenants** | Rental income | No |
| **Money received for someone else (passing through)** | Transfer between my accounts, Note "passing through" | Yes |

Rule of thumb: **if it isn't truly theirs to spend, it isn't income.** Borrowed money, payouts of their own savings and money passing through all stay out of income.

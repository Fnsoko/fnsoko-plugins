# Teaching a Fnsoko money class

Format: **6 weekly sessions of about 2 hours**, or **one full day + a check-in 30 days later**. Each session works on a laptop (planner + Claude) or on a printed one-page worksheet for anyone without one.

## Class persona: Amina (fictional)

Nobody shares real numbers aloud. Everyone follows Amina first, then does their own privately. Her statements are in `tests/fixtures/statements/amina/`, so you can run a live import in session 2.

| | |
|---|---|
| Take-home pay | KSh 95,000, paid on the 28th |
| Accounts | Equity (bank), M-Pesa |
| Moves | Bank → M-Pesa twice a month |
| Regulars | KPLC tokens, Naivas, Netflix, Uber, airtime |
| Unknown | Pays "JOHN K" 3 times a month (family support) |
| Saving | 10,000 a month standing order to a money market fund |
| Surprise | M-Pesa and bank fees add up to about 260 a month |

## Sessions

| # | Session | Big idea | Ladder | Tabs | In class | Number to write down | Homework |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Know my numbers | Cashflow = in − out; income, expenses, savings, debt | 1 | My Money Profile, Setup | Amina's profile together, then their own 5 rounds (paper or Claude); label KNOWN / ESTIMATED / UNKNOWN | Money in − money out | Download last month's statements and save unlocked copies |
| 2 | Where does it go? | Needs vs wants; leaks; transfers aren't income | 1 | Setup tags, My Rules, Money Log | Sort 20 sample M-Pesa lines on paper; live import of Amina's statements; write 3 rules | Biggest want | Import their own statements; answer Claude's questions |
| 3 | Does it add up? | The log vs real balances | 1 → 2 | Month-End Check, My Money Ladder | Month-End Check on Amina (VERIFIED), then their own | Money not logged | Fix gaps; start the starter buffer |
| 4 | Protect myself | Buffer → expensive debt → full fund | 2-4 | My Emergency Fund, My Loans | Fund target; avalanche vs snowball on a sample loan list | Months of cover; debt-free date | Automate a transfer, however small |
| 5 | Budget on purpose | 50/30/20; lifestyle creep | 5 | My Budget, My 50-30-20 | Their own split; "you got a 20% raise" exercise | Future % | Cap one want |
| 6 | Grow and plan ahead | Goals, net worth, retirement | 6-7 | My Goals, My Net Worth, My Future | One goal with a date; net worth; "retire at 60" | Retirement gap | Book a monthly money date |

## Worksheet pattern (one page per session)

1. **Today's idea** in one sentence (English + Swahili line).
2. **Amina's example**, worked.
3. **Your turn**: 5-8 boxes to fill.
4. **One number to write down.**
5. **This week's homework** (one action).
6. Safety reminder in the footer.

When asked for a worksheet, produce it in this pattern for the session requested.

## Facilitator notes

- Start each session with the **ladder**: "Where are you on the ladder?" Hands up for steps 1-7.
- Session 2 is where it clicks: show the "money not logged" figure on Amina, then let them find their own.
- Some will have Fuliza or betting lines. Treat it matter-of-factly in class; take sensitive conversations aside.
- Not everyone has a Claude plan with skills. Point them to the **Ask Claude** prompts at the bottom of Start Here (see `fallback-prompts.md`), which give the same outputs.
- Say every session: never share PINs, passwords, statement passwords or account numbers; never let AI guess amounts; check the maths yourself; save approved numbers in the planner.
- **Measuring impact** (only with consent): the ladder step at session 1 and at +30 days, and the share of months that are VERIFIED. Use the opt-in usage log or a show of hands; never collect amounts.

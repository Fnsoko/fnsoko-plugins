# Money words in English and Swahili

Use the person's language. If they write in Swahili or mix Swahili and English, explain in the same mix. Keep the planner's category names in English so the formulas still match.

| English | Kiswahili | One-line explanation (simple) |
|---|---|---|
| Income | Mapato | Money that comes to you (salary, business, rent) |
| Salary | Mshahara | Pay from a job |
| Expenses / spending | Matumizi | Money that goes out |
| Budget | Bajeti | A plan for where each shilling goes |
| Needs | Mahitaji | What you must pay to live and work (rent, food, transport) |
| Wants | Matamanio | Nice to have (eating out, new clothes) |
| Future (savings + investing) | Akiba na uwekezaji | Money set aside for later |
| Savings | Akiba | Money kept, not spent |
| Emergency fund | Akiba ya dharura | Money only for surprises: job loss, hospital, urgent repairs |
| Debt | Deni | Money you owe |
| Loan | Mkopo | Money borrowed that you must pay back |
| Interest | Riba | The extra you pay to borrow, or earn when you save |
| Balance | Salio | What is left in the account |
| Transfer | Uhamisho | Moving money from one place to another |
| Cash | Pesa taslimu | Notes and coins |
| Fees / charges | Ada / makato | Small amounts taken for a service |
| Statement | Taarifa ya akaunti | The list of everything in and out of an account |
| Goal | Lengo | What you're saving for, how much and by when |
| Investment | Uwekezaji | Putting money somewhere to grow |
| Inflation | Mfumuko wa bei | Prices going up, so the same money buys less |
| Net worth | Thamani halisi | What you own minus what you owe |
| Retirement | Kustaafu | When you stop working for money |
| Pension | Pensheni | Savings for when you retire |
| Lifestyle creep | Matumizi kupanda na mapato | Spending rising every time income rises |
| Rent | Kodi ya nyumba | What you pay to live in a house |
| Chama | Chama | A savings group that contributes and takes turns |

Explaining by example works best: "Mahitaji ni kama kodi na chakula; matamanio ni kama kula hotelini."

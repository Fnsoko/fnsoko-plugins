# Money maths and facts that change

Run calculations in code. State every assumption. Label results ESTIMATED.

## Formulas

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| The leak | money in − every known outflow (spending, savings, debt). A positive figure nobody can name = untracked spending |
| Month-End Check | should have = start + money in (incl. from savings, borrowed) − money out − cash withdrawals; not logged = should have − actually have |
| Emergency fund target | months (3-6) × monthly essentials (Needs) |
| Months of cover | emergency fund ÷ monthly essentials |
| 50/30/20 | Needs ÷ money in, Wants ÷ money in, Future (savings + investments) ÷ money in. Loan repayments count as Needs |
| Lifestyle creep | growth in Wants vs growth in money in, this month vs the average of the 3 months before |
| Yearly rate from monthly | about monthly × 12 (simple); true cost (1 + monthly)^12 − 1 (e.g. 7.5% a month ≈ 90% simple, 138% compounded) |
| Goal monthly amount | (target − saved) ÷ months left (no returns unless asked) |
| Future value | saved × (1 + r/12)^(12n) + monthly × ((1 + r/12)^(12n) − 1) ÷ (r/12) |
| In today's money | future amount ÷ (1 + inflation)^n |
| Income a pot can pay | pot × withdrawal rate (default 4%) ÷ 12 |
| Pot needed | monthly income wanted × 12 ÷ withdrawal rate |
| Monthly saving needed | (pot needed in future money − saved × (1 + r/12)^(12n)) × (r/12) ÷ ((1 + r/12)^(12n) − 1) |
| Debt order | Avalanche = highest rate first (least interest). Snowball = smallest balance first (quick wins). Their choice |

## Defaults (assumptions, not facts)

| Assumption | Default | Say |
|---|---|---|
| Investment return | 10% a year | "An assumption; real returns go up and down." |
| Inflation | 5% a year | "Close to the Central Bank of Kenya's target." |
| Withdrawal rate | 4% a year | "So the money can last about 30 years." |
| Expensive debt | 20%+ a year | "Most mobile loans and credit cards." |
| Starter buffer | 1 month of essentials | |
| Full emergency fund | 3 months (steady income) to 6 months (variable income, dependants) | |

## Facts that change

Never present these from memory as current. If web search is available, look them up and give the date and source. If not, say "check the current figure" and give where to check.

| Fact | Where to check |
|---|---|
| Tax bands, PAYE reliefs | KRA |
| NSSF and SHIF rates | NSSF, SHA |
| T-bill and bond rates | Central Bank of Kenya |
| Money market fund yields | The fund's factsheet; CMA list of approved funds |
| Inflation | KNBS / Central Bank of Kenya |
| M-Pesa charges | Safaricom tariff page |
| Mobile loan rates | The lender's app terms |
| Exchange rates | The person's bank or remittance app on the day |

When you use a changing fact, write it as: "T-bills were about X% (CBK, [date]). Check before deciding."
